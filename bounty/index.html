How to enter

  1. Own the game. Get SAVE LOST ANGELES (coming soon). It’s yours to keep either way. Entering the bounty is free.
  2. Sign in on this page with the account that bought it.
  3. Press ENTER THE BOUNTY. Pick your state, confirm you’re 18+ and agree to the rules.
  4. Record and play. Start your screen recorder (or stream) on this page, then play the Full City Run on NATIVE.
  5. Submit your video link here. The crew reviews claims in the order they come in.

What your video must show

  • This page first, with your gamertag and ENTERED badge. Then straight into the game.
  • The whole run, start to finish, with the on-screen run timer visible. No cuts, no edits.
  • The Full City Run results screen: CONTINUES 0 and the RUN row NATIVE · 1P · GOLDEN BOY.
  • YouTube (unlisted is fine), Google Drive, or a Twitch VOD. Anyone with the link must be able to watch it.

Cheats, console edits, modified games, macros, slow-mo or someone else playing = disqualified. The crew checks every claim by hand before anything is paid.
