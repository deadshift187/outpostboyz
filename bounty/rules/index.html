01Sponsor

The $100 Bounty (the “Bounty”) is run by Outpost Boyz (“Sponsor,” “we,” “us,” “the crew”). Contact: squad@outpostboyz.com. Website: outpostboyz.com/bounty.

By entering, you agree to these Official Rules and to the Sponsor’s decisions, which are final on every matter about the Bounty.

02What this is (and isn’t)

  • This is a game-of-skill contest. The winner is decided only by skill: beating the game under the rules below. There is no random drawing, no luck, and no odds.
  • There is no entry fee. SAVE LOST ANGELES costs $4.99. That price is for the game, which is yours to keep and play whether or not you ever enter. The price is the same for everyone and does not change because of the Bounty. Entering the Bounty costs nothing extra.
  • Owning the game is required because the Bounty is played in the game. You do not get more entries, more chances, or any advantage by paying more, buying again, or tipping.

03Who can enter

You can enter only if all of these are true when you enter and when you win:

  • You are 18 or older and have reached the age of majority where you live.
  • You are a legal resident of the United States (the 50 states or Washington, D.C.) and you are physically in the US when you play your bounty run.
  • You are not a resident of Arkansas, Arizona, Connecticut, Delaware, Iowa, Indiana, Louisiana, Maine, Montana, South Carolina, South Dakota or Tennessee. The Bounty is not open to residents of those states.
  • You bought SAVE LOST ANGELES on your own Outpost Boyz account at outpostboyz.com, and that purchase has not been refunded or charged back.
  • You play on your own account. One person, one account. Duplicate or shared accounts are disqualified.

Not eligible: Outpost Boyz crew members; anyone who helped make, test or balance the game; the immediate family (spouse, parents, children, siblings) and household members of any of them; any account with admin access; and any account that was given the game for free (a “comped” or granted account) — even if that account also bought it.

The Bounty is void where prohibited or restricted by law.

04Dates

  • The Bounty starts when the status on outpostboyz.com/bounty changes to OPEN. The opening date is posted on that page. Before then it shows “Opens soon” and no entries or claims are accepted.
  • The Bounty ends at the earlier of: (a) the moment a winner is verified and announced, or (b) 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time on the day 12 months after the opening date.
  • Claims must be submitted while the Bounty is open. If nobody wins before it ends, no prize is awarded.

05How to enter

  1. Buy SAVE LOST ANGELES at outpostboyz.com/games/save-lost-angeles and set a gamertag on your account.
  2. While signed in, go to outpostboyz.com/bounty and press ENTER THE BOUNTY. Choose your state and confirm that you are 18+, a US resident, and that you agree to these rules.
  3. Play your bounty run and record it (see Proof).
  4. Submit your video link with the claim form on the bounty page.

Only runs played after you entered count. Playing the game without entering is never an entry.

06How to win

Be the first eligible entrant to complete SAVE LOST ANGELES’ FULL CITY RUN on the NATIVE tier, as 1 player, with GOLDEN BOY as your hero, with zero continues, in one sitting, and to prove it under these rules.
  • Full City Run means the game mode that plays every level and boss in order in one go and ends on the Full City Run results screen. Level-select clears do not count.
  • NATIVE is the difficulty tier named NATIVE in the game. Extra lives picked up in normal play are allowed.
  • 1 player means no second player joins at any point. If a second player drops in, the run does not count.
  • GOLDEN BOY must be the hero for the whole run. Changing hero restarts the run.
  • Zero continues means you never use a continue. The results screen must show CONTINUES 0. Game over ends the attempt.
  • One sitting means one continuous run from the start of the Full City Run to its results screen, without quitting, reloading or restarting. Pausing is fine; the recording must keep rolling.
  • The run must be played on the official game at outpostboyz.com, unmodified, signed in to the account that entered.
  • “First” means the earliest submitted claim that passes review. Claims are reviewed in the order they were received.

07Proof

Your claim needs a link to one unedited recording of the whole run: a screen recording or a livestream VOD. YouTube (unlisted is fine), Google Drive, Twitch VOD or another link the crew can open without an account. It must stay available until the Bounty is decided.

  • Start the recording on outpostboyz.com/bounty showing you signed in with your gamertag and your ENTERED status, then go to the game without stopping.
  • Show the whole run from the Full City Run start to the Full City Run results screen, including the on-screen run timer throughout.
  • Hold on the results screen long enough to read it: TOTAL TIME, CONTINUES 0, and the RUN row reading NATIVE · 1P · GOLDEN BOY.
  • No cuts, edits, speed changes, overlays that hide the game, or splices. Game audio on is preferred. A phone filming the screen is OK if everything is readable.
  • A livestream counts if the full VOD is saved and linked.

We may ask for a short live play check (for example, playing part of the game on a stream) before deciding a claim.

08Claims

  • Submit claims only through the claim form on the bounty page while signed in to your entered account. Emailed claims are not accepted.
  • Up to 3 claims per account per 24 hours. Submitting a claim does not make you a winner.
  • Your claim status (pending, approved or rejected, with a reason) shows on the bounty page.

09Review and verification

  1. The crew watches the full recording and checks it against these rules and the game.
  2. If it passes, we email the address on your Outpost Boyz account. You must reply within 7 days with: your legal name, date of birth, state of residence, mailing address, a PayPal account in your own name, and a photo of a government photo ID showing your name, birth date and state (cover the ID number). You will also sign a short declaration that you are eligible, you played the run yourself, and you followed these rules.
  3. If you don’t reply in time, can’t be verified, or aren’t eligible, the claim is rejected and we review the next claim in order.
  4. A claim is only a win once we mark it verified. The winner’s gamertag and date go on the Beat Pile at outpostboyz.com.

10Fair play and disqualification

Not allowed, and grounds for disqualification of the claim and the account:

  • Modified, patched or unofficial copies of the game; browser console commands, scripts, extensions, cheats or memory edits.
  • Macros, turbo, bots, tool-assisted or pre-recorded input, or anything that slows down or speeds up the game.
  • Anyone other than the account holder playing any part of the run, or account sharing.
  • Using a bug to skip a level or boss, become invulnerable, or change the game’s rules. Normal movement tricks are fine. Not sure? Email us before you run.
  • Edited, spliced or borrowed recordings, false information, or harassment of the crew or other players.

We may disqualify any entrant or claim we reasonably believe broke these rules or tampered with the game, the site or the Bounty. Our decisions are final.
