Connect your Twitch channel

Your viewers' Bits, subs and raids will change the game while you play. About 2 minutes.

What you need

  • Your Twitch channel name, the part after twitch.tv/
  • An Outpost Boyz game open in Chrome, Edge or Firefox on your computer
  • Your streaming app (OBS, Streamlabs...). See Put the game on your stream
  • Optional: you logged in to Twitch in the same browser (only for follows and channel points)

Steps

  1. Open the game on outpostboyz.com and click LIVE MODE on the start screen.
    SCREENSHOT: game start screen with the LIVE MODE button circled
  2. Tick Twitch (you can have TikTok and YouTube on at the same time).
    SCREENSHOT: LIVE SETUP with the Twitch row ticked
  3. Type your channel name, only the name. For twitch.tv/coolgamer, type coolgamer.
  4. Click Connect. After a few seconds you'll see Connected to #yourname.

    No password is needed for this part. The game only reads your chat; it never posts anything.

    SCREENSHOT: green "Connected" status
  5. Click Test a cheer. Something should happen in the game. That means everything works.
  6. Coming later: follows and channel points. They need a Twitch account login, which this version of the game doesn't have yet; everything else works without it. When it arrives you'll click Connect Twitch account. Twitch opens a page asking to let "Outpost Boyz Live" see your followers and channel point redemptions. Check that it is really twitch.tv in the address bar, then click Authorize. You come straight back to the game.

    You'll need to do this again now and then when Twitch logs it out. The game will tell you.

    SCREENSHOT: Twitch "Authorize" page
  7. Close the settings panel and start playing. Your viewers can now cheer, sub, gift subs and raid to change the game.

Troubleshooting

"Couldn't find a Twitch chat"

Check the spelling. Use only letters, numbers and underscores, and no @ or link.

It says Connected, but cheers do nothing

Did you capture the same window you're playing in? A Browser Source is a second copy of the game. See Put the game on your stream.

Follows or channel points stopped working

They aren't in this version yet (see step 6). Later: Twitch logged the game out; click Connect Twitch account again. If it says "missing permission", you logged in with the wrong account. Use the channel owner's account.

"Couldn't reach Twitch chat"

Check your internet, or a work or school firewall. Try another network.

The game freezes when I click on OBS

Don't minimize the game or cover it completely. Keep it on a second screen, or side by side.

House rules

Bits change the game only. Never offer prizes, money or random rewards for Bits. Twitch doesn't allow Bits as a bet or wager.
