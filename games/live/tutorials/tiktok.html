Connect your TikTok LIVE

Gifts, likes, follows and shares from your viewers change the game while you play. It takes about 10 minutes the first time and 1 minute after that.

What you need

  • A Windows PC (on a Mac, use Plan B)
  • TikFinity, a free app made by a third party, not by us or TikTok
  • A TikTok account that is allowed to go LIVE, and your @username
  • The game open in Chrome or Edge (Firefox works too; Safari does not)
  • Your streaming app (TikTok LIVE Studio or OBS). See Put the game on your stream

Plan A: TikFinity (easiest)

  1. Go to tikfinity.zerody.one and download the Desktop App for Windows. Install it like any other program.
    SCREENSHOT: TikFinity download button
  2. Open TikFinity, sign in, and type your TikTok @username.
  3. Go LIVE on TikTok (LIVE Studio or your phone), then click Connect in TikFinity. It should say it's connected to your LIVE.

    Keep TikFinity open the whole time you stream. You can minimize it.

    SCREENSHOT: TikFinity showing "connected" with your username
  4. Open the game on outpostboyz.com, click LIVE MODE, tick TikTok, then pick Plan A: TikFinity.
    SCREENSHOT: game Live mode, TikTok, TikFinity option
  5. Click Connect. Your browser will show a small box near the address bar asking if outpostboyz.com can connect to apps on this device or devices on your local network.
    Click Allow. This lets the game talk to TikFinity on your own computer. It doesn't share anything online.
    SCREENSHOT: Chrome permission pop-up with the Allow button circled
  6. You'll see Connected to TikFinity. Click Test a gift, and something should happen in the game.
  7. Close the settings panel and play. When viewers send a Rose, tap likes, follow or share, the game reacts.

Plan B: Euler Stream key (Mac, or if Plan A won't connect)

Euler Stream is a free third-party service. You make your own free account and paste its key into the game. The key stays in your browser; we never see it.

  1. Go to eulerstream.com, make a free account (the "Community" plan costs $0 and needs no card), and copy your API key from the dashboard.
    SCREENSHOT: Euler dashboard with the API key area (blurred)
  2. In the game: LIVE MODE, tick TikTok, then pick Plan B: Euler key. Type your TikTok username without the @, and paste the key.
  3. Go LIVE on TikTok, then click Connect, then Test a gift.

Keep your key secret. Don't show the settings screen on stream. If a key ever leaks, make a new one in Euler and delete the old one.

Stay within TikTok's rules

  • Be there. Play, talk and react. TikTok can stop paying streams that run by themselves with nobody present.
  • Gifts are a bonus, so don't beg for them nonstop.
  • Never promise prizes, money or random rewards for gifts.
  • You have to be 18 or older to go LIVE and receive gifts.
  • TikFinity and Euler are unofficial tools. You use them at your own risk.

Troubleshooting

I clicked "Block" by mistake

Click the icon at the left of the address bar, then Site settings. Find Local network access (it may be called Apps on device) and set it to Allow. Reload the page.

No pop-up appeared, and it says "Can't reach TikFinity"

Check that TikFinity is open and connected. Try Chrome or Edge instead of Safari. If the game is inside another website's frame, open it directly on outpostboyz.com.

TikFinity is connected, but gifts do nothing

Are you capturing the same window you play in? A Link source or Browser Source is a second copy of the game. See Put the game on your stream.

Plan B says "not live"

Start your TikTok LIVE first, then click Connect. Check the username: no @, no link.

Plan B says "key missing" or "too many requests"

Paste the key again. The free plan has a daily limit, so try again later or use Plan A.

The game freezes when I click on another app

Don't minimize the game or cover it completely. Keep it on a second screen, or side by side.
